# Surface brief: Streetlight startsida

Scope: index.html, one-page Swedish site. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: new potential sponsors/donors in Sweden. Understand the work in under a minute, trust a small association, become a sponsor (fadder) or donor.
Proof and content: 20 sponsored children, what is paid for (school fees, uniform, materials, medical costs), support for parents' own income-generating business, local authorities in Addis Ababa select the children, two organisations with two independent boards. No photos, quotes, figures or payment details supplied: placeholders only, labelled.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Swedish. WCAG AA. Brand guide supplies colours, Open Sans, flame symbol, Panton wordmark (not available; Nunito Black Italic stands in until the real logo file is supplied).
Memorable moment: the lamp head on the spine travels down the page and lights each section; 20 small flames light one by one.
Unresolved: payment details, photos, stories, real logo file, contact address.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a street at night with one lamp post as its spine; hope is light that reaches each section in turn. Refuses the charity template (hero photo of a child, donation-tier cards, big impact numbers).
OWN-WORLD: Asphalt #424242 ground with a fixed darker lamp-post gutter on the left; Primary yellow #FFD200 for light, the flame symbol and drenched sections; white text; green #B8E986 only as the Etiopien/second-organisation colour. Open Sans Light for headlines, semibold/bold for subheads. Flame symbol drawn as SVG. Soft radial pools of light on asphalt, no cards.
STORY: Visitor understands that 20 children from very poor families get schooling and their parents get a way to earn a living, with local authorities choosing the children; believes it because it is small, specific and structured on two boards; acts via Bli fadder.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left gutter with lamp post and flame head; wordmark top-left of content; vision line as 5-6rem Light headline over the left 7 columns; one-sentence description; primary yellow button Bli fadder plus text link Så arbetar vi; right side a large lamp SVG whose cone of light lands in a yellow pool on the road; fixed Bli fadder pill top right.
