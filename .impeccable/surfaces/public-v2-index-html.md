# Surface brief: Streetlight V2, Vägmärken i natten

Scope: public/v2/index.html, a one-page Swedish site, built as an alternative variant beside V1 (public/index.html) for comparison. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: new potential sponsors/donors in Sweden. Understand the work in under a minute, trust a small association, become a sponsor (fadder) or donor.
Proof and content: same facts as V1 (PRODUCT.md). No photos, quotes, figures or payment details supplied: placeholders only, shown as covered signs.
Assets: real logotype and flame symbol extracted as vectors from the supplied brand style guide PDF (public/v2/assets/).
Memorable moment: the signs are retroreflective; the visitor's pointer (scroll position on touch) is a light that makes them shine; 20 delineator reflectors light up one by one.
Unresolved: payment details, photos, stories, contact address.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a road at night lined with signs; each fact is a sign plate that shines when light reaches it. Refuses the charity template (child hero photo, tier cards, impact counters) and V1's single lamp-post spine.
OWN-WORLD: Asphalt #424242 ground with a darker road strip and white/yellow lane markings; large flat sign plates in brand yellow #FFD200 with an inset asphalt border line and rounded corners, asphalt text; green #B8E986 plate only for Etiopien; red #FF1736 only as the tape on covered (not-yet-valid) signs. Open Sans 700 for sign lettering, 300 for long-form display on asphalt. Arrows, route shields, delineator posts, posts and brackets. No cards, no shadows; light is radial glow and plate sheen.
STORY: Visitor reads the vision on a lit sign, follows the route (20 children, school, family, Addis Abeba authorities, two organisations), believes it because each sign is specific, and takes the Bli fadder arrow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Real white logotype top-left; nav as small route plates top-right. Left 60%: a huge yellow sign plate on two posts carrying the vision H1 in bold sign lettering, lit by a streetlight arm entering from top-right whose pool lands on the plate. Below the plate: a one-sentence lead on asphalt and the primary action as an arrow-shaped yellow direction sign "Bli fadder →" plus a quieter "Så arbetar vi ↓". Road strip with dashed lane marking at the viewport bottom.
FORM: Vägmärken i natten (Swedish road signage at night), position 4 of 7 on the ordered list, seed: ce610d81860f5027
