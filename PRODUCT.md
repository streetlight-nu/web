## Stack
Plain static HTML/CSS (user's choice). No framework, no build step.

## Users
Primary: new potential sponsors and donors in Sweden who do not yet know Streetlight. They arrive to understand the work, decide whether to trust a small association, and become a sponsor (fadder) or give a gift. Secondary (confirmed as served, not primary): existing sponsors and members who want to follow progress, find reports, pay, or make contact.

## Product Purpose
Streetlight is a non-profit association registered in Sweden and Ethiopia. It supports 20 sponsored children from very poor families in Addis Ababa. Vision: spread hope of a bright future to the children and their families. It believes education is a way out of poverty and supports schooling: school fees, uniform, school materials and medical costs if a child falls ill. It also supports the children's families: parents can receive help and financial support to start an income-generating business. It works with local authorities in Addis Ababa, who help select children from vulnerable families, making Streetlight part of Ethiopia's social safety net. Two organisations, one in Ethiopia and one in Sweden, with two independent boards; the Swedish association supports the Ethiopian work financially. Success: a visitor understands this in under a minute, trusts it, and becomes a sponsor or donor.

## Positioning
Small and specific: 20 children, real local partners, support that covers the child's schooling and the family's own livelihood, with the municipal authorities in Addis Ababa choosing the children. Not a large aid brand.

## Operating Context
Swedish-language site. Swedish association funding an Ethiopian sister organisation; two independent boards.

## Brand Commitments
Brand style guide supplied (Streetlight - Brand style guide.pdf).
- Vision (sv): "En värld där människor sprider hopp om en ljus framtid".
- Logotype: wordmark "Streetlight" in Panton extra-bold italic (FontFabric), with a yellow flame replacing the dot of the i. Dark asphalt background symbolises the struggles people face (poverty, lack of resources and opportunity); the yellow light symbolises hope of a bright future, spread one person at a time. Versions: white on asphalt, asphalt on white with yellow flame, one-colour.
- Symbol: a flame/light shape, yellow on asphalt, yellow on white, or asphalt on white.
- Type: Open Sans (H1 light 40px, H2 semibold 30px, H3 bold 20px caps, body regular 16px).
- Colours: Primary yellow #FFD200, Accent red #FF1736, Secondary green #B8E986, Asphalt black #424242, White #FFFFFF.
- The user said the guide is inspiration with the street-lamp and light as theme, and that strict adherence is not required: freedom to try new expressions.

## Product Principles
1. Light is the metaphor and the mechanism: the page reveals itself as hope spreading, never as pity.
2. Dignity first: children and families are people with futures, not victims; no poverty imagery as decoration.
3. Trust through specificity: concrete numbers (20 children), concrete costs covered, named partners, transparent structure.
4. One clear action: becoming a sponsor must be reachable at any point without hunting.
